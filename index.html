Employees can request pay data by gender

🇳🇴🇮🇸🇱🇮 EEA Countries Also Affected
Norway, Iceland, and Liechtenstein are not EU members but participate in the European Economic Area (EEA). The Pay Transparency Directive must be incorporated into the EEA Agreement before these countries transpose it into national law. Timeline may differ from EU member states, but similar requirements will apply. This tracker focuses on EU-27 member states only.
📋 Methodology note: "No information currently" indicates that no publicly available transposition documentation was found through official government portals, EUR-Lex, or established legal trackers. This does not mean a country is taking no action: preparatory work, internal consultations, or draft legislation may be underway without public documentation. Status can change rapidly; corrections and updates are welcome.
